Nordic winters and El Niño strength

Winter temperature, precipitation, snow, frost and thaw metrics in Finland and the other Nordic countries, grouped by the ENSO phase of each winter (NOAA Oceanic Niño Index). Compares super El Niño winters with ordinary El Niño, neutral and La Niña winters. Everything on this page is computed in the browser from the bundled data.

How to use this page
